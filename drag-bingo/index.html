What's the deal

The show.

Drag Bingo at the Bowlo combines the classic game everyone knows with the glamour, comedy and high-energy performances of live drag. Expect plenty of laughs, cheeky banter, dazzling costumes and unforgettable moments as the queens take to the stage between bingo rounds.

Hosted by the unstoppable and always glamorous Vollie LaVont, each show features fabulous guest drag performers delivering spectacular performances throughout the night.

Of course, it wouldn't be bingo without prizes galore. Whether you're feeling lucky, coming for the entertainment, or just looking for a great night out — there's something for everyone.
