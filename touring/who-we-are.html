The Gflam Story

Who we are.

Seasoned industry veterans with over fifty years of combined experience.

Gflam Touring may be new by name, but our founders are seasoned main-stays — clocking up an impressive fifty years of combined experience in live events and entertainment touring.

We are the full package — a one-stop-shop with instinctual flair for live entertainment, consumer engagement and much more.

Our secret? Crafting authentic, unforgettable experiences that leave everyone on a high.

Created during COVID by two industry friends who shared a passion: to make a difference to the Australian music industry and create a better industry for everyone.
