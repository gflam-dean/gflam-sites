Questions clubs ask

Straight answers.

Do we pay anything?

No. The room, a date, the bar open and the show listed on your website and in your members newsletter: that is the club's whole side of it. We sell the tickets and [keep the ticket money]; you keep the bar, the kitchen and gaming.

How many people will come?

We quote Tugun Bowls Club's figures as Tugun's: 223 tickets on the first night and 113 on the second. Nobody can promise a crowd, which is why we do all the marketing and send you the report afterwards. Each show pulled a largely new crowd, mostly women booking a table together, and three quarters of them local.

Is it family friendly?

[18 and over, or all ages: Dean to confirm.] The humour is cheeky, the bingo is real, and the room is welcoming. Hens groups, birthday tables and bowls club regulars sit side by side.

Do we need a permit?

That depends on your state. The show has run in Queensland under Tugun's arrangement. For a club in New South Wales or Victoria we check the state's bingo rules before any cash prize is promised, and we do it before the date is announced, not after. Until it is confirmed for your club, prizes are prizes. [Per-state answer to be filled from the October 2026 legal note.]

What if it does not sell?

[If fewer than 100 tickets are sold two weeks out, we tell you and you can cancel with no cost.] It is your Saturday night and your room, so the risk should sit with us, not you.

Dates for [March to June 2027] are being set in November and December. One show per area per date, so the first club in each town gets its suburb to itself.
