About the Show

What to expect.

Drag Bingo at the Bowlo is unlike anything you've experienced. Your host for the evening, Vollie LaVont, makes sure of that — from the moment you walk in, you're part of the show.

Multiple rounds of bingo are played throughout the evening, with drag performances, audience interaction and hilarious banter filling every moment in between.

Whether it's a birthday, girls' night or just a Tuesday you want to remember — this is the answer.
