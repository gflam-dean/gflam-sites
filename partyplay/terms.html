Short version: you are buying software to run games at your own private party. We supply the game. You supply the party, the prizes and the music.

What you are buying

A licence to run PartyPlay games on the dates you nominated at purchase. Play runs from midnight on your first day until 6am the morning after your last, in your own state's time. Building your games is not limited by those dates and costs nothing extra.

Your licence is capped at 50 players. There is no upgrade beyond that.

Private use only

PartyPlay is for a private gathering. It is not for a business, a club, a venue, a fundraiser or a promotion of any kind, and it must not be used to run a game that anybody pays to enter.

If you are running games at a pub, club or venue, or for a business in any way, you need VenuePlay instead. It is built for that, and it handles the licensing rules that come with it.

We may end a licence without refund if it is used commercially. Nobody pays to enter a PartyPlay game: the product has no way to sell a ticket or take an entry fee, and that is deliberate.

Prizes

Any prize is yours to provide and yours to hand over. We are not involved in it, we never hold it, and we do not run a lottery or any game of chance for money.

Music

PartyPlay does not supply music or lyrics. Where a game involves music you play it yourself from your own source. A private party at your home does not need a music licence, which is another reason this is private use only.

Refunds and changing your date

You can move your date free of charge, as many times as you need, any time before your party starts. Your code and your games stay exactly as they are. Most people who think they want a refund actually want a different date, and that costs nothing.

If you do want your money back:

  • Within 7 days of buying, and more than 24 hours before your party: a full refund, no questions.
  • Any time up to 24 hours before your party: a credit for the full amount, good for 12 months on any future party.
  • Inside the last 24 hours, or once it has started: no refund and no credit. By then we have held the date for you and you have had the software.

Both of those stop 24 hours before your party, whichever else applies. If you book three days out, the seven day window still closes when the party gets close, not afterwards.

None of this limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. If PartyPlay does not work as described, you are entitled to a remedy regardless of anything written here, and we will sort it out.

What we do not promise

PartyPlay needs a working internet connection and a device to play on, and we cannot promise anything about your wifi, your television or your guests' phones. We do our best to keep the service up, but we do not guarantee it will be uninterrupted.

Your guests

Guests give a nickname and nothing else. You are responsible for what happens at your own party, including anything your guests type in.
