Practice run

A rehearsal, with three guests we made up. Press the buttons, see what lands on the telly and what a guest sees on their phone. Then do the real one in front of people.

Your clock is not running. Nothing here touches your party, nothing is saved, and none of your time is used. Practise as often as you like.

What the telly shows

--
Press Call a number to start

On the night this is a separate screen: you open partyplay.com.au/tv on the television and it follows along by itself. Your phone stays in your hand as the remote, exactly like the buttons above.

What your guests see

Three pretend guests, with real tickets built the same way the game builds them. Watch a line fill up. When somebody is one number away you will see it here, and on the night they will be shouting about it.

Happy with it? Back to your party. The clock starts when you press Start the party there, and not before.
