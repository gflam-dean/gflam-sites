Questions

Do we need to download anything?

No. Guests open a web page in whatever browser is already on their phone. That is the whole thing.

What if we do not have a smart TV?

A cable from a laptop works and always will. So does a laptop or an iPad propped up where people can see it. Here are the five ways.

How long do we get?

From midnight on your day until 6am the following morning, in your own state's time, so a party that runs late is not cut off mid game. Three day licences run the same way across all three.

Can I set the games up in advance?

Yes, and it costs nothing extra. Take as long as you like. Only playing is tied to your dates.

What if the date changes?

Move it, free, as many times as you need. Your code and your games stay exactly as they are.

How many people can play?

Fifty. If you need more than that you are having a bigger night than this is built for.

What do you do with our details?

Guests give a nickname and nothing else, and it is deleted when your licence expires. We never collect their contact details. The full version is short.
