Before you ask Questions

Do we need to download anything?

No. Guests open a web page in whatever browser is already on their phone. That is the whole thing.

Can we put it on more than one screen?

Yes, as many as you like, and it costs nothing extra. Open the same screen link on each one and they all show the same thing at the same moment. Handy when the kitchen cannot see the telly.

What if we do not have a smart TV?

A cable from a laptop works and always will. So does a laptop or an iPad propped up where people can see it. Here are the five ways.

How long do we get?

24 hours, or 3 days, from the moment you press start. Not from when you buy it, so a party that kicks off late is not eating into your time.

Can I set the games up in advance?

Yes, and it costs nothing extra. Building games does not start the clock. Only pressing start does.

What if the party gets moved, or does not happen?

Nothing to do, and nothing lost. You never told us a date, and your licence keeps for 12 months.

How many people can play?

Fifty. More than that and you are having a bigger night than this is built for.

What about photos?

There is a camera on every guest's phone and a video button under it. It all lands in one album you can download or share, and the whole lot is deleted 30 days after your party.

What do you do with our details?

Guests give a nickname and nothing else. We never collect their contact details. The full version is short.
