# Songs the 8 September curation removed, that plenty of people know

256 songs with half a million listeners or more, sitting in the library in NO pack.
**252 of them were in a pack until 8 September**, when the curation that was meant to cut
the duds cut these as well. Ranked most listened first.

This is the other half of the question, and it turned out to be the bigger half: not
"what should we cut" but "what did we cut that we should not have". We spent today
hunting 129 songs nobody knows while 256 songs people love sat unplayable.

Some are still defensible for a pub. Radioheads Let Down is not a singalong and Last
Christmas has a season. That judgement is yours: the number only says people know it.

Mark the ones you want back. A row tagged VERSION may be the wrong recording, in which
case the plain one goes back instead.
