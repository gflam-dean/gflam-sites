# Terms of Service changes, 28 October 2026

For Dean to approve BEFORE release. Branch fix/legal-gaps-28-oct, file venueplay/terms.html.

Why: the legal compliance matrix of 28 Oct 2026 (gaps 2 and 3, conflicts C1 and C2) found two places where the live Terms contradict your standing rulings: section 6 called musical bingo "regulated gaming" and a "gaming activity", and 6.5 (Victoria) read as needing a permit before ANY bingo, musical bingo included, free or paid. Wording checked against Anisimoff Legal 9380, 8 Oct 2026 letter, point 1(a) (s8.4.4, the warranty and indemnity), point 3 (s8.4.7B, paper tickets in VIC), point 7 (s8.2.4A, free bingo needs no permit) and the 1 Oct 2026 letter, points 3 and 9 (indemnity; "VenuePlay is not responsible for or in any way involved with the conduct or organisation").

Every other clause is unchanged, word for word. The customer-is-conductor clause (6.2) and its sentence "VenuePlay is not responsible for or in any way involved with the conduct or organisation of the gaming activity" are kept exactly; the opening paragraph now also says it of "any game you run", so musical bingo and trivia stay covered after they leave the definition of gaming activity.

The onboarding tick sentence (index.html, nsw.html, onboard.html, hq.html, the billing Worker's VPA_COMPLIANCE_TICK) does not mention musical bingo or call anything regulated, so it is unchanged. No email repeats section 6.

## 2. Section 6, opening paragraph

BEFORE

> This section is the heart of the agreement, so it is numbered. In it, "gaming activity" means bingo, musical bingo, raffles, members draws and any other game of chance you run on VenuePlay. Bingo, musical bingo, raffles and members draws are regulated gaming in every Australian state and territory. Trivia is a game of skill and is not a gaming activity.

AFTER

> This section is the heart of the agreement, so it is numbered. In it, "gaming activity" means number bingo, raffles, members draws and any other game of chance you run on VenuePlay. Number bingo, raffles and members draws are regulated gaming in every Australian state and territory. Musical bingo is a music game played on a grid of songs, not bingo, and trivia is a game of skill, so neither is a gaming activity. You still run them: you conduct every game at your venue, whatever its format, VenuePlay is not responsible for or in any way involved with the conduct or organisation of any game you run, and 6.9, 6.13 and 6.14 apply to every game you run on VenuePlay.

## 3. 6.5 Victoria

BEFORE

> 6.5 Victoria. If your venue is in Victoria, you warrant that before you run bingo (including musical bingo) or a raffle that charges for entry you hold a current minor gaming permit from the VGCCC, or you are a VGCCC-declared community or charitable organisation that has notified the VGCCC, and that you will not use VenuePlay to issue bingo tickets to players' phones. Victorian law bans supplying bingo tickets or a random number generator to anyone without a permit, so we rely on this warranty to supply the software to you at all.

AFTER

> 6.5 Victoria. If your venue is in Victoria, you warrant that before you run number bingo that charges for entry, or a raffle that charges for entry, you hold a current minor gaming permit from the VGCCC, or you are a VGCCC-declared community or charitable organisation that has notified the VGCCC, and that you will not use VenuePlay to issue number bingo tickets to players' phones. Free number bingo, where nobody pays to play, needs no permit (section 8.2.4A of the Gambling Regulation Act 2003 (Vic)), and in Victoria it is played on printed tickets: VenuePlay calls the numbers and shows the game on your screen, and no ticket is issued to a phone. Victorian law bans supplying bingo tickets or a random number generator to anyone without a permit for bingo that needs one, so we rely on this warranty to supply the software to you.
