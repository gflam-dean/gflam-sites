Good to know

Questions

When does VenuePlay launch?
VenuePlay launches August 2026. Founding venues get first access, and we'll be in touch before then to get you set up.
What does "founding venue" actually mean?
The first 100 venues to sign up lock in 20% off for life — $2.40 per seat a month instead of $3. Once 100 spots are gone, founding pricing closes for good.
Do players need to download an app?
No. They scan a code and play straight from their phone's browser. No app, no login, no fuss — which means more of the room actually joins in.
What kind of venues is it for?
Pubs, clubs, bowls clubs and RSLs — anyone running a regular entertainment night, or wanting to start one. If you've got a screen and a crowd, you're set.
How does billing work?
A simple subscription priced per seat, per month, based on your venue's max seats. Pay monthly at $2.40 a seat, or yearly for $2 a seat — founding pricing is locked for life either way. Nothing is charged until launch in August.
Who's behind it?
VenuePlay is built by Gflam Touring, who run live drag bingo and entertainment nights across Australia. It's the same show experience, built into a platform any venue can run.
