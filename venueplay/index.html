We're Gflam Touring. Good to know

Questions

When does VenuePlay launch?
VenuePlay launches September 2026. Founding venues get first access, and we'll be in touch before then to get you set up.
What does "founding venue" actually mean?
The first 100 venues to sign up lock in 20% off for life: $2.40 per player a month instead of $3. Once 100 spots are gone, founding pricing closes for good.
Do players need to download an app?
No. They scan a code and play straight from their phone's browser. No app, no login, no fuss, which means more of the room actually joins in.
What kind of venues is it for?
Pubs, clubs, bowls clubs and RSLs: anyone running a regular entertainment night, or wanting to start one. If you've got a screen and a crowd, you're set.
How does billing work?
A simple subscription priced per player, per month, based on your venue's max players per session. Pay monthly at $2.40 a player, or yearly for $2 a player, and your founding pricing is locked for life either way. Your first month is free from the September launch, then billing recurs at your founding rate. Cancel any time before your first payment and you are never charged.
What if more players turn up than my plan?
No problem and no surprise fees. Set your max to your usual room. If a night runs bigger, the extra players are simply added at your per-player monthly rate, so your plan grows to match what you actually use. The screen flags it the moment you go over.
What do I need to run it?
A screen, an internet connection, and a way to get the display onto that screen: a TV with a browser, a cheap streaming stick, or an HDMI cable from a laptop or tablet all work. Players bring their own phones. For a rock-solid night we'd point you toward a stick or a cable over wireless casting, since that doesn't lean on venue wifi.
Is there a lock-in contract?
No lock-in. It's a rolling subscription you can cancel anytime. Your founding 20% discount stays locked for as long as you're with us, and if you cancel before your first payment, you're never charged.
What support do I get?
Founding venues get hands-on onboarding to get your first night running, plus ongoing support if anything comes up. The whole thing is built so your bar staff can run it without a tech in the room.
Can players win prizes?
Yep, you set the prizes, exactly like you do now. Bar tabs, meat trays, members jackpots, vouchers, whatever suits your venue. VenuePlay runs the game and calls the winners; the prizes are yours to decide.
Is players' data safe?
Players join with just a name, no account, no login, nothing they have to hand over. Anything captured is opt-in and handled under Australian privacy rules. We don't sell player data, full stop.
Who's behind it?
VenuePlay is built by Gflam Touring, who run bingo, live music and entertainment across Australia. It's the same show experience, built into a platform any venue can run.
