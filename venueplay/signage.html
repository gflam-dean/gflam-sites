Print your scan to play signs

Put one on every table. The QR code goes to your venue, not to one night's game, so the same sign works every week and for every format you run. Print it once and leave it out.
The bit after the slash in your screen link, venueplay.com.au/tv?your-venue
Filled in for you once we find your venue.
Name your nights: "Trivia Thu 7pm. Bingo Sat 2pm".
Say it however your room says it. "Scan to play bingo", "Join the quiz".
Leave it as it is, or put your own words in.
Leave blank and the sign stays generic.
Print settingsChoose A4, portrait, and turn ON background graphics so the pink prints.
Office printer or print shopOffice printer keeps a 6mm white margin, which is all a desktop printer can do. Print shop prints to the edge. The dark Ticket and the dark Spotlight are near-solid ink, so send those to a print shop.
Table cardsCut along the dashed lines. A6, so four fit on one page.
Table tentsCut the page in half, then fold each half across the middle. The top is printed upside down on purpose so it reads from both sides of the table.
Can’t scan?Every sign also prints your permanent 6-character code, so anyone can type it in instead.
