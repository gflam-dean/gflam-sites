Floor staff troubleshooting
📱
A player cannot join
Check they scanned the code on the TV, or typed the 6 characters shown on it. Make sure their phone is on the venue wifi or has mobile data. A quick refresh of the page clears most stuck joins.
✅
Handling a BINGO claim
When a claim comes in, the host's iPad shows that player's card with the called numbers marked. Verify the line before you call it out. If it is not complete, tap Play on and the game carries on. Nothing shows as a win on the TV until the host confirms.
👍
A number did not mark
Players mark their own numbers by tapping them, but the TV board shows every number called regardless. If someone missed a tap they have lost nothing, the host checks the real card at claim time, so a missed mark never costs the win.
This is a self-contained training tool. It runs a pretend game on this page only, with no sign in and no internet game needed, so it always works in the room. The live product connects the three screens over the network.
