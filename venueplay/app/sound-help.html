Get the sound through your speakers

Musical bingo plays from your TV. To fill the room, send the TV's sound to your venue's speakers. Find the setup that looks like yours and follow the steps. Most take five minutes.

TV to a soundbar (HDMI ARC)

Your soundbar or amp has an HDMI socket marked ARC or eARC.

  1. On the back of the TV, find the HDMI socket marked ARC or eARC. Only one socket has it.
  2. Plug an HDMI cable from that socket into the soundbar's socket marked ARC, eARC or TV.
  3. On the TV remote, open Settings, then Sound, then Sound output. Pick HDMI ARC, Receiver or External speaker.
  4. Turn on HDMI-CEC if the TV asks (Samsung calls it Anynet+, LG calls it SimpLink, Sony calls it Bravia Sync).
  5. Play a song on the console and turn the soundbar up.

TV to an amp (Optical cable)

Your amp has a small square socket marked Optical, Digital In or TOSLINK.

  1. On the back of the TV, find the small square socket marked Optical or Digital Audio Out. Pull out the little cap if there is one.
  2. Plug an optical cable from it into the amp's Optical or Digital In socket. It only fits one way round.
  3. On the amp, pick the input called Optical, Digital or TV.
  4. On the TV, open Settings, then Sound, then Sound output, and pick Optical.
  5. Play a song on the console and turn the amp up slowly.

TV to an amp (Headphone socket)

Your amp only has red and white plugs, or a socket marked AUX.

  1. Get a cable with a small headphone plug (3.5mm) on one end and red and white plugs on the other. Any electronics shop has them.
  2. Plug the small end into the TV's headphone socket, and the red and white plugs into the amp's AUX or Line in, red to red, white to white.
  3. On the amp, pick AUX or Line in.
  4. On the TV, open Settings, then Sound, and set the headphone output to Line out or Fixed if it offers it. Turn the TV's volume to about halfway.
  5. Play a song on the console and set the volume on the amp.

No headphone socket on the TV? Use the optical setup with a small optical to red and white converter.

TV to a speaker (Bluetooth)

You have a Bluetooth speaker or soundbar, and no cable run to it.

  1. Put the speaker into pairing mode (usually hold its Bluetooth button until the light flashes).
  2. On the TV, open Settings, then Sound, then Sound output, and pick Bluetooth speaker list or Bluetooth audio.
  3. Pick your speaker from the list. It says Connected when it's done.
  4. Play a song on the console and set the volume on the speaker.

Keep the speaker within about 10 metres of the TV, with nothing big in between. For a big room, a cable to your amp is more reliable.

A laptop to the amp

You run the TV screen from a laptop, or your TV has no way out to the amp.

  1. Plug the laptop into the TV with an HDMI cable, for the picture.
  2. Plug a headphone to red and white cable from the laptop's headphone socket into the amp's AUX input, for the sound.
  3. On the laptop, choose where the sound goes: on Windows click the speaker icon by the clock and pick Headphones; on a Mac open System Settings, then Sound, then Output, and pick Headphones.
  4. Set the laptop's volume to about three quarters and use the amp to make it louder.
  5. Play a song on the console to check.

Still no sound?

  1. Is the amp or soundbar on the right input (HDMI ARC, Optical, AUX or TV)? This is the usual fix.
  2. Is the TV muted, or its volume at zero? With a headphone cable, the TV's volume controls the amp too.
  3. Did the TV's sound output setting change back? Some TVs reset it when they're turned off. Set it again.
  4. Swap the cable for another one. Cables fail more often than TVs.
  5. Turn the TV and the amp off at the wall for ten seconds, then on again, TV first.
  6. Play a YouTube video on the TV. If that has no sound either, the problem is the TV to amp connection, not venueplay.
