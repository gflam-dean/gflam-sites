Hosts

People who can run games on the night. Add a mobile and tick which venues they can host. They sign in at venueplay.com.au/app with a text code, no app to install.

Managers

For groups: a manager runs and sets up the venues you give them. Tick what each can do. They sign in at venueplay.com.au/app with a text code. Billing and the player-data rules below stay with you.

Player data & name display

What players are asked for when they join, and how their name shows on the TV (John S, J Smith or John Smith). Opens that venue's settings.

Marketing opt-ins

Players who ticked "keep me posted" when they joined. Download them as a CSV for your own marketing.

Members draws

Set your members draw once and it runs every week. The live jackpot and the next draw show on your TV automatically.

Your members draws

The draw day comes from your start date, and the current jackpot is worked out from the start date, starting jackpot and weekly increase. You can still adjust the jackpot if you need to.

Taking it off the TV clears the draw day and time, so it stops appearing between games. The draw, its jackpot and your members list are all kept, so putting a night back on brings it straight back.

Advertising

Upload your own promo images to play on the venue TV between games, then open venueplay.com.au/tv on your screen. Changes appear within a few seconds.

Venue logo

Shows in the top-left corner of your TV, over games and between them. Use a PNG with a transparent background, and make sure the logo itself is white or a bright colour. The TV screen is dark, so a black or dark logo will disappear.

No logo
Slides

Each slide is one image. Set how long it stays on screen, and optional dates so a promo ends itself (leave the dates blank to show it all the time). Up to 12.

Table signs

Print a "Scan to play" sign for your tables. The QR code goes to your venue, not to one night's game, so the same sign works every week and for every game you run. Choose an A4 poster, table cards four to a page, or fold-over table tents.
