A new draw. Fill it in and save it.

Set by the draw: it resets when the jackpot is claimed and grows by the weekly increase when it rolls over.

The draw day fills in from your start date (change it if your draw runs on another day). Leave the maximum blank for no maximum; once it is reached, the weekly increase goes into a reserve that seeds the next jackpot.

Your members list
Loading your members list…

This is personal information your venue holds about your members. Keep it to a member number and a name, and take someone off when they ask.

Upload your members list

Choose a CSV file from your club system or Excel (File, Save As, CSV). Next you pick which column is the member number, the first name and the last name.

Which column is which?

Pick which column holds each one. We fill the box below for you to check.

Weekly reminder

Would you like a weekly reminder to update your members list? We can email you.

Or paste your members

One per line as number, name (e.g. 142, Jane Smith). We add any new numbers to your members list.

Fix a name

A pasted name is split at the first space, so "Mary Anne Smith" is stored with the surname "Anne Smith" and that is what goes on the TV when she wins. Fix it here without taking her off the list.

Take someone off the list

Remove a member by their number. Past wins stay on record.

Permits and authorities for draws

Record any permit, licence or authority your venue holds for raffles and members draws, so it is on file. We do not check it, and recording it does not make a draw lawful: that stays with you.
