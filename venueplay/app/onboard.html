Onboard a venue
Creates the venue, its default settings and one TV screen. The venue slug becomes the TV web address venueplay.com.au/tv?<slug>, which is what every email and text we send actually uses.
Venue
Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces. Must be unique. Auto-filled from the name and postcode, edit if needed. There are around 100 Royal Hotels in Australia, so the postcode goes on the end the same way it does when a venue signs up itself.
Four digits, required. It sets the venue's state, which decides which gaming rules they are shown and whether phones may hold a bingo ticket, and it keeps the slug unique.

Independent (card)
A venue must attach to exactly one billing parent. Grouped venues are invoiced through an operator group. Independent venues are card-billed through their subscription record.
Groups are set up in the admin. If a group is missing, add it first.
The independent venue's paid subscription record. It is created when they sign up on the page (Worker, service role), so the browser cannot create it here. Copy its id from the Supabase Table Editor (venueplay_). The player cap is read from that record's max_seats.
Compliance (required)

First manager (optional)
Enter their mobile and we set up their login the same way the venue would: they sign in at venueplay.com.au/app with a text code, no app to install. Leave it blank to add staff later from Your account.
Australian mobile. They get advertising, draws and raffles, player opt-ins and add-hosts; billing and the player-data rules stay with the account owner.
