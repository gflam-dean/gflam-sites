Start a Jag the Joker jackpot

Each week a ticket is drawn and that person picks one face-down card. Find the Joker and win the jackpot. Anything else stays turned over and the jackpot carries to next week.

The Joker's card is picked at random by the VenuePlay server the moment you press start, and sealed. Nobody can see or move it, including us. A fingerprint of it goes on the screen straight away, so when the Joker is found everyone can check it was there from the start.

This is a game of chance with a prize. Check your state's rules for running it before you start.
