"Live now" means a hosted game session is open (in the lobby, running or paused). Click Watch TV to open that venue's TV screen in a new tab and check the game is really showing. This reads open sessions from vp_sessions; broadcast bingo and members draws do not open a session, so a venue running only those will not appear here.
Venues that have gone quiet
VenueQuiet forLast game
Sorted shortest quiet first, because a venue that missed one week is the one you can still win back. Last game counts every format: sessions for trivia, musical bingo and raffles, and game reports for broadcast bingo, which opens no session. Bingo is recorded from the first ball, so a night the host never formally ended still counts. A venue that has never run a night is measured from the day it was set up. We only look back a year, so an older venue with nothing in that window reads over a year rather than never.
Venues that have left
A venue whose owner has cancelled is archived automatically 30 days after their paid period ends, so departed venues stop sitting on the active lists. It never touches a venue that is still paying, still inside its paid period, or that has run a night since cancelling. Check first, then run it.
Everyone who has asked to leave
One screen for every venue that has cancelled, whether it is still inside its paid period or long since archived. The reason and the words they typed come from the cancellation itself, so this is what they actually said, not a summary. Mark one Contacted once you have rung them, and leave a note for whoever picks it up next. Both are stored against the venue and show here for good.
Group nights nobody has billed
A venue in a group has no billing account of its own, so nothing meters it: the host is never shown the overage screen and nothing is charged. That is deliberate (groups are invoiced by hand), but until now nothing told you WHAT to invoice. This is that list: every night a grouped venue went past its plan, priced at $2 a head, the same figure the metered path uses. Nothing here has been charged or even asked for.

Signups whose email does not look like a venue land here (a trivia host, a personal Gmail). They can run games on team names only and collect no player data until you Approve. "No opt-ins" records your decision and leaves them running games on team names, just without collecting player data.
Paper play, last 60 days
WhenVenueWhat happened
A trivia night that asked a lot of questions with almost no phones answering, or a musical bingo night where paper cards were printed and the host said none were played. Paper players only count towards a venue's bill once they are checked or scored, so these are the nights worth a friendly call.
Venues who want to hear from us
Venues who ticked "send me the occasional update" when they signed up. This is our mailing list, nothing to do with a venue's own players. Only the ones who actually ticked it are in the file, because under the Spam Act that tick is the consent.
Remove a player who has asked
Type the whole email address or mobile number they gave the venue. You will see every venue that holds it before anything is removed. Where a venue has downloaded its list since they joined, we email that venue asking them to delete the person from their own copy as well.
Player data collection, every account
AccountEmailVenuesCollectingDecision
An account on its own venue-looking domain approves itself. Everyone else waits here. Automatic means nobody has decided: the database works it out from their email domain on each save, and lets them through on their own venue-looking domain. Approve opt-ins overrides that as a yes, Never as a no. Note that "bar" is not one of the words that auto-approves, so The Mini Bar and a Sports Bar sit on Automatic and stay blocked even on their own domain.
