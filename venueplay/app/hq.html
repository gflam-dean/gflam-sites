Venues that have gone quiet
Venues that have left
A venue whose owner has cancelled is archived automatically 30 days after their paid period ends, so departed venues stop sitting on the active lists. It never touches a venue that is still paying, still inside its paid period, or that has run a night since cancelling. Check first, then run it.
VenueQuiet forLast gameState
Sorted shortest quiet first, because a venue that missed one week is the one you can still win back. Last game counts every format: sessions for trivia, musical bingo and raffles, and game reports for broadcast bingo, which opens no session. Bingo is recorded from the first ball, so a night the host never formally ended still counts. A venue that has never run a night is measured from the day it was set up. We only look back a year, so an older venue with nothing in that window reads over a year rather than never.
Group nights nobody has billed
A venue in a group has no billing account of its own, so nothing meters it: the host is never shown the overage screen and nothing is charged. That is deliberate - groups are invoiced by hand - but until now nothing told you WHAT to invoice. This is that list: every night a grouped venue went past its plan, priced at $2 a head, the same figure the metered path uses. Nothing here has been charged or even asked for.
