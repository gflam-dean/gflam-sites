How it runs tap for a step-by-step
  1. Pick your winning pattern, then tap Open lobby. The TV shows a join code.
  2. Players scan it, grab their tickets, and you'll see them land in the players list.
  3. Tap Start game, then call each number. The TV reveals the ball and players daub their cards.
  4. When someone gets the pattern, their claim drops into your queue. Tap to confirm the win.
  5. Set the next pattern or prize to carry on, or tap End game to finish.
