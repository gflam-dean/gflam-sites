    The look

    Dark space, bright neon, nothing extra. Stage black with one or two neon highlights. Pink is the signal colour, so use it sparingly. Yellow is for winning moments only. If you want another colour, the answer is more space, not more colour.

    How we sound

    • Short sentences. One thought each.
    • Specific beats generic: "Three rounds in 45 minutes", not "engaging gameplay".
    • Read it out loud. If you would not say it to a publican at the bar, rewrite it.
    • Confident, not boastful. Aussie without the parody.

    Photos and posts

    • Real people in real rooms: the cheer when a winner is called, phones in hands, your screen lit up.
    • Warm and a little dark is good. Pub light is the look.
    • Five hashtags at most. No stickers.
    • Never stock photos, and never AI-made pictures.

    The logo

    • Pick one of the versions above. Never build your own.
    • Do not recolour, stretch, outline or tilt it.
    • On a photo, use the white version.
    • Leave clear space around it, about the height of the play mark.

    Before you hit send

    • Only contact people who ticked the opt-in box.
    • Do not promise a prize the venue has not confirmed.
    • Say what is on and when: "Trivia, Tuesdays 7pm". Free to play, bring your phone.
