    Do

    • Say what is on and when: "Trivia, Tuesdays 7pm".
    • Use "powered by VenuePlay" under your own venue name.
    • Keep clear space around the logo, about the height of the play mark.
    • Tell people it is free to play and they only need their phone.

    Do not

    • Recolour, stretch, outline or tilt the logo.
    • Put the pink logo on a busy photo. Use the white one.
    • Promise a prize you have not confirmed with the venue.
    • Contact anybody who has not ticked the opt-in box.
