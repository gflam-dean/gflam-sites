Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how venueplay, a product of Gflam Group Pty Ltd (ABN 35 679 383 049), trading as venueplay ("venueplay", "we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, discloses and protects your personal information. It applies to venueplay.com.au and the venueplay platform.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). By using our website or signing up, you agree to this policy.

1. Who this policy is about

venueplay is a platform sold to venues (pubs, clubs, bowls clubs and similar) to run trivia, musical bingo, bingo, raffles, members draws, jag the joker and punters reckon on their own screen. This policy covers the venues and staff who use venueplay, the players who join its games from their own phone, and the club members whose club uploads them for a members draw. Players can always play with just a name. What each person is asked for, and who gets it, is set out in section 2.

2. What we collect

When a venue signs up or enquires

  • Venue name and the number of venues you operate
  • Contact email address
  • Your name and mobile number (when you sign up we text a sign-in code to the mobile; on an enquiry the mobile is optional)
  • Postcode
  • Your chosen plan (monthly or annual) and your maximum players per session
  • Anything you tell us in the contact form or by email

When we contact a venue about venueplay

We contact pubs and clubs about venueplay using business details from public sources and business lists, such as the venue's name and address and a contact's name, role and work email or phone. The links in our emails let us see which venue visited our site from them. We do this only for business contacts, never to identify a player, and we stop if you ask.

When a host or staff member signs in

Hosts and venue staff sign in with a code we text to their mobile. We keep their name, mobile number, the venue they work for and their role there, so we can let them in and show the venue who has access. A venue can also add a login for whoever does its marketing, who may be from outside the venue. That login sees the names and contact details of players who asked for the venue's offers only if the venue allows it.

When you contact our help desk

When you email us, send us a help or contact form, or call us and we note the call, we keep your name, how you contacted us, the venue it is about, what you told us and our replies. We use it to answer you and to keep track of what was asked. Only venueplay staff can see it.

When you sign up for our monthly email

If you ask for our monthly email on our website, we keep your email address, your venue's name and postcode, and when, where and how you signed up, including the country and state your connection comes from. We use them to send you that one email a month, to make it right for your venue's state, and to see which pages people sign up from. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Payment information

Card and payment details are collected and processed directly by Stripe, our payment provider. We do not see or store your full card number. We keep your billing status and the references Stripe gives us, so we can manage your subscription.

Players

When someone joins a game's lobby on their phone, they enter a name. There is no password and nothing to log in to. Their phone shows a short notice that playing means they agree to venueplay's terms and this privacy policy, with a link to this page.

Every player is asked for a first name and a mobile number, and a venue can also choose to ask for some or all of the following. Every one of these fields is optional for the player, and a game can always be played by giving a name alone:

  • Last name
  • Postcode
  • Email address
  • Whether they want offers from that venue (one tick box, off by default). Ticking it asks for offers from that venue and nothing else; it does not ask their age.

Where a state's law keeps under-18s out of a game, the player's phone asks them to confirm they are 18 or over before they can play. It is asked only for those games in those states, never for trivia or musical bingo. The answer stays on the player's phone and is not sent to us or to the venue.

The venue owns its copy of what players give there. A venue gets only the details it asks for, and we hold its copy on its behalf so it can see and export its own list. The venue, and the people it gives access to, can see that list. A business that runs several venues on one venueplay account can see the list of each of its venues. Each venue is its own business and handles the details it gets under its own privacy policy. We do not sell player data, we do not use it to market venueplay, and we never pass one venue's players to another business, to a visiting host, or to anyone else, apart from a sale or restructure of venueplay (section 3).

Your venueplay player profile. When a player gives a first name and a mobile number, venueplay keeps a light player profile for them, so they are not asked to type the same details again at any venue on venueplay. It holds their first name and mobile and, if they gave them, their last name, email address and postcode, with a few notes such as which venues already have their details and when they last played. It does not hold a history of the games they played. A player who gives only a name, or no mobile, has no profile. Their phone remembers the profile so it can be recognised next time. The profile is venueplay's, separate from any venue's copy, and we use it only to fill in a player's details when they join a game.

When another venue asks for a first name, last name or postcode the player gave before, we fill it in for them, so they do not type it again. A remembered phone shows "Playing as" with the player's first name and part of their mobile number. A venue that does not already have that number gets it only if the player ticks that venue's offers box. An email address the player gave before is shown to them, partly hidden, with "Use this". It goes to another venue only if the player taps "Use this" and ticks that venue's offers box.

One mobile number per player. If a remembered phone is not theirs, the player taps "Not me" and the phone shows the empty join form, as for a new player. Where the join screen offers "Not my number", a player can change their number with a code we text to the new one, and we update it on their profile and on the venue lists their profile filled in. Otherwise a player can ask us to correct it, as set out in section 9.

We keep a player's profile until the player asks us to delete it, even if a venue they played at leaves venueplay. To have it deleted, ask us at venueplay.com.au/help or email hello@venueplay.com.au with the mobile number on it, and we will delete it, apart from anything we must keep by law.

Marketing contact only happens where the player has ticked the box, and only from the venue they were playing at. A player who wants to come off a venue's list, or wants to know what a venue holds about them, can ask that venue, or contact us at the address in section 14. We will remove their details from that venue's list, and where the venue has already downloaded a copy, we tell the venue so they come off that copy too. Coming off a venue's list and deleting a player profile are separate: ask for both and we will do both.

Purchase raffles. At a venue's purchase raffle, staff enter each entrant's details on the venue's own device: the details that venue asks for, such as name, mobile and postcode. An entrant who asks for offers from the venue joins that venue's list. Entry details are kept for the venue's records and are deleted on request.

Coaster Trivia. A player answering a Coaster Trivia round on their phone is asked for a first name and a mobile. At a venue on venueplay, these go to that venue, like any other sign-up there. At a venue that is not on venueplay, venueplay keeps them for that venue's scores only: they are never shown to any venue, never put in an export and never used for marketing. A player's first name can appear on that venue's Coaster Trivia scores. A player can ask us to delete their details at any time.

Feedback at the end of a game. When a game ends, a player can rate it with one tap. The rating holds no name or contact details. A player can also tell us more and, if they choose, leave an email address so we can reply. That email goes only to our help desk: it is not given to the venue, not added to any venue's list and never used for marketing. Venues never see who left a rating.

What a player's phone sends us without being asked. So that one phone is counted once, and so that games stay fair, the game keeps a random identifier in the player's own browser, and we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of the phone's internet address rather than the address itself. Neither is used for advertising and neither is given to the venue.

Members lists. A club running a members draw can upload its own members list: a member number and a name for each member. Those people may never use venueplay themselves. The list belongs to the club, is held for that club alone, is used only to run and record its draws, and is never used for marketing by us or shared with anyone else, apart from a sale or restructure of venueplay (section 3). A member who wants to come off it should ask their club, or contact us at the address in section 14.

If a club wishes to, it can also upload each member's email, mobile, postcode and whether they want offers from the club. We hold these for the club, as part of its members list.

When a player at that club gives a mobile that matches a member on its list, we note for that club that the player is a member.

We keep a venue's copy of its players' details, and its members list, while the venue holds an active venueplay account. Some time after a venue closes its account, we delete the names and contact details on its player list and members list. The record of each game, which can include the names players used and who won, and purchase raffle entries are kept with the venue's records. A player's own venueplay player profile is not affected.

Sensitive information. We do not ask for sensitive information, such as health, religion or political views, and we ask that you do not send it to us.

Technical information

Like most websites, we automatically collect basic technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you visit and the site you came from. We use Google Analytics for this. We also run the Meta Pixel on our public pages, so we can show venueplay ads to venues who have looked at the site. Neither is on the host console, the venue screens or the player pages. The Meta Pixel never receives a venue’s player list, or anyone’s name or email address.

Google Analytics also uses Google’s advertising features. These estimate an age range and a gender for visitors, and recognise the same person across their devices, using what Google already holds for people signed in to a Google account. We only ever see this as totals for the whole site, never as an individual. You can turn it off for yourself at Google’s opt-out page or in your Google ad settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To set up and provide the venueplay service to your venue
  • To process your subscription and payments through Stripe
  • To provide support and respond to your enquiries
  • To send you service messages about your account, billing and the platform
  • To send you marketing about venueplay where you have consented or the law allows it, which you can opt out of at any time
  • To keep a player's venueplay player profile, so they are not asked to type the same details again
  • To improve and secure the platform
  • To meet our legal obligations

We may also:

  • Use these details to run and improve venueplay, and to add new games and features
  • Contact players and venues by email, text message or app notification about their account or a game they joined. Marketing from us is sent only with consent (section 4), and you can opt out at any time
  • Use service providers to run venueplay for us, such as hosting, payments, messaging, analytics and support tools, some of them overseas (sections 5 and 6)
  • Share details with a business we are related to, or with a buyer if venueplay or part of it is sold or restructured, who must keep to this policy
  • Use information that does not identify anyone, such as totals, for any purpose

4. Marketing and your consent

We only send marketing where you have agreed to receive it, or where the Spam Act 2003 (Cth) allows it, such as to a venue's published business address. Every marketing message includes a way to unsubscribe. Opting out of marketing does not stop essential service messages about your account or billing.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers we use to run venueplay, and only so they can do their job for us. These may change over time. Today they are:

  • Stripe: payment processing and subscription billing
  • Supabase: database hosting
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, delivery and security
  • Resend: sending email
  • Mobile Message and ClickSend: sending text messages
  • Google and Meta: analytics and advertising on our public pages

We may also share information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights, safety or property. Legal requests should be sent to support@venueplay.com.au.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some of our providers store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States, the European Union and other countries where they operate. Where this happens we take reasonable steps to ensure your information is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

7. How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as we need it to run venueplay, or as the law requires. Section 2 says what happens to a venue's player details when it leaves. A player can ask us to delete their venueplay player profile at any time (section 9).

9. Accessing, correcting and deleting your information

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is wrong or out of date, or ask us to delete your venueplay player profile. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to confirm who you are first, and if we cannot do what you ask, we will tell you why.

10. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar browser storage to make the site and games work and, on our public pages, to measure how the site is used. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as well without them.

11. Children

venueplay is a business tool intended for venue operators. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first using the details below and we will work to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page, with its effective date shown at the top. Where the law requires it, we will also tell venues and players about a significant change.
