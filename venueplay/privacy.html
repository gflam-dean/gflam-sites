Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how venueplay, a product of Gflam Group Pty Ltd (ABN 35 679 383 049), trading as venueplay ("venueplay", "we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, discloses and protects your personal information. It applies to venueplay.com.au and the venueplay platform.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). By using our website or signing up, you agree to this policy.

1. Who this policy is about

venueplay is a platform sold to venues (pubs, clubs, bowls clubs and similar) to run trivia, musical bingo, bingo, raffles, members draws, jag the joker and punters reckon on their own screen. Most of the personal information we handle relates to the venue operators and staff who sign up and use the platform. Players join a game from their own phone and can always play with just a name. A player who gives a first name and a mobile number is remembered by venueplay, so they are not asked to type the same details again at any venue on venueplay. What a player is asked for, and who gets it, is set out in section 2. Clubs may also upload their own members list for a members draw, which is covered in section 2 as well.

2. What we collect

When a venue signs up or enquires

  • Venue name and the number of venues you operate
  • Contact email address
  • Your name and mobile number (when you sign up we text a sign-in code to the mobile; on an enquiry the mobile is optional)
  • Postcode
  • Your chosen plan (monthly or annual) and your maximum players per session
  • Anything you tell us in the contact form or by email

If you start an account with "Play a quick game" and add a card, we keep the email address you typed on Stripe's card page as a note on your account. We send no account email to it unless you confirm it is yours.

When we contact a venue about venueplay

We contact pubs and clubs about venueplay using business details from public sources and business lists: the venue's name, address and postcode, and a contact's name, role and work email or phone. Each link in our emails is unique to the venue we sent it to, so we know which email helped and which pages were opened on our site afterwards, using a cookie that lasts 90 days. We do this only for business contacts and never use it to identify a player, and we stop if you ask. If your browser sends Do Not Track, we do not record your visit.

When a host or staff member signs in

Hosts and venue staff sign in with their mobile number. We send a one-time code to that number by SMS, and entering the code signs them in, so there is no password to remember. We keep their name, their mobile number, the venue they work for and their role there, so we can let them in and show the venue who has access. The codes are sent through our SMS providers (see section 5), and each code works once. A host is signed out after four hours.

When you contact our help desk

When you email us, send us a help or contact form, or call us and we note the call, we open a help desk ticket. It holds your name, the email address or phone number you contacted us from, the venue it is about if there is one, what you told us, and our replies and notes. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of what was asked and how we answered. Tickets are read only by venueplay staff. A venue, a host or a player cannot see any ticket, including one they sent. We keep tickets with our other customer records, for as long as section 8 allows.

When you sign up for our monthly email

If you ask for our monthly email on our website, we keep your email address, your venue's name and postcode (and the state that postcode is in), the page you signed up on and any campaign tags in its address, when you signed up and the words you agreed to, and the country and state your connection comes from (never your internet address). We use them only to send you that one email a month, so that anything in it is right for your venue's state, and to see which pages people sign up from. Every email has an unsubscribe link, or you can write to us at the address in section 14.

Payment information

Card and payment details are collected and processed directly by Stripe, our payment provider. We do not see or store your full card number. We store the billing status and the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers Stripe returns to us, so we can manage your subscription.

Players

When someone joins a game's lobby on their phone, they enter a name. There is no password and nothing to log in to. When a player joins, their phone shows a short notice that playing means they agree to venueplay's terms and this privacy policy, with a link to this page. Once a player has a venueplay player profile (see below), the notice is shown again only when its words change.

Every player is asked for a first name and a mobile number, and a venue can also choose to ask for some or all of the following. Every one of these fields is optional for the player, and a game can always be played by giving a name alone:

  • Last name
  • Postcode
  • Email address
  • Whether they want offers from that venue (one tick box, off by default). Ticking it asks for offers from that venue and nothing else; it does not ask their age.

Where a state's law keeps under-18s out of a game, the player's phone shows a separate screen asking them to confirm they are 18 or over before they can play. It is asked only for those games in those states, never for trivia or musical bingo. The answer stays on the player's phone for that night and is not sent to us or to the venue.

The venue owns its copy of what players give there. A venue gets only the details it asks for. We hold the venue's copy on that venue's behalf so the venue can see and export its own list. We do not sell player data, we do not use it to market venueplay, and we never pass one venue's players to another venue, to a visiting host, or to anyone else.

Your venueplay player profile. When a player joins a game and gives a first name and a mobile number, venueplay keeps a light player profile for them, so they are not asked to type the same details again at any venue on venueplay. It holds their first name and mobile and, if they gave them, their last name, email address and postcode, plus the venue where the profile began, which venues already have their mobile or email from them, when they saw the notice above, and when they last played. It does not hold a history of the games they played. A player who gives only a name, or no mobile, has no profile. Their phone keeps a random key so we can recognise it next time, and we keep only a scrambled form of that key. The profile is venueplay's, separate from any venue's copy, and we use it only to fill in a player's details when they join a game.

When another venue asks for a first name, last name or postcode the player gave before, we fill it in for them, so they do not type it again. A remembered phone shows "Playing as" with the player's first name and part of their mobile number, and no box to type a mobile. A venue that does not already have that number gets it only if the player ticks that venue's offers box; otherwise the venue does not get it. An email address the player gave at one venue is shown to them, partly hidden, with "Use this" and "Different email". It goes to another venue only if the player taps "Use this" and ticks that venue's offers box; otherwise they can type a different email or leave it blank.

One mobile number per player. A player's profile holds one mobile number, and a remembered phone has no box to type a different one. If the phone is not theirs, the player taps "Not me": the phone forgets the profile and shows the empty join form, as for a new player. If they then give a first name and a mobile, that venue gets them and venueplay starts a new profile for that phone; the earlier profile is not changed and is kept until someone asks us to delete it. Changing a number from the phone is not always available. Where the join screen offers "Not my number", we text a code to the new number. Once the code is entered we update the number on that player's profile, and on the player and sign-up rows their profile created at the venues that already had the old number. Rows made before we linked them to the player's profile are not changed. They keep the old number until they are removed under the normal retention described in section 2. We keep a record of each request: the new number, the last three digits of the old one, a scrambled form of the code we texted, how many times the code was sent and tried, a scrambled form of the internet address it came from, and when it was asked for and changed. We keep that record until the player's profile is deleted, and it is deleted with it. Where the join screen does not offer "Not my number", a player can ask us to correct their number, as set out in section 9.

We keep a player's profile until the player asks us to delete it, even if a venue they played at leaves venueplay. To have it deleted, ask us at venueplay.com.au/help or email hello@venueplay.com.au with the mobile number on it. A person on our team deletes it for you within 30 days, apart from anything we must keep by law.

Marketing contact only happens where the player has ticked the box, and only from the venue they were playing at. A player who wants to be removed, or wants to know what a venue holds about them, can ask that venue, or contact us at the address in section 14. We will remove their details from that venue's list. Where the venue has already downloaded a copy of its list, we also tell the venue, so that they are removed from that copy too. Coming off a venue's list does not delete a venueplay player profile, and deleting a profile does not take a player off a venue's list. Ask for both and we will do both.

Each box a player is asked to fill in is on the join screen, and the first time a player joins a game on venueplay they see a short notice with a link to this policy. What a venue asks for, and who gets it, is set out in section 2.

Purchase raffles. At a venue's purchase raffle, staff enter each entrant's details on the venue's own device: the details that venue asks for, such as name, mobile and postcode. An entrant who asks for offers from the venue joins that venue's list, and an entrant already on the venue's list is linked to it. Entry details are kept for the venue's records and are deleted on request.

Coaster Trivia. A player answering a Coaster Trivia round on their phone is asked for a first name and a mobile. At a venue on venueplay, these go to that venue, like any other sign-up there. At a venue that is not on venueplay (one a player added, or one from our own lists), venueplay keeps the first name and mobile for that venue's board, once per mobile. We keep them for venueplay only: they are never shown to any venue, never put in an export and never used for marketing. If a venue a player added is still waiting to be confirmed as a real venue after 30 days, it is deleted with these details. Otherwise we keep them until the player asks us to delete them, in the same way as a player profile. Wherever a round is played, the first name shown on that venue's Coaster Trivia scores is removed after 35 days.

Feedback at the end of a game. When a game ends, a player can rate it with one tap. The rating holds no name or contact details. After "Good" or "Not for me" they can tell us more, and, only if they leave "Email me about this" ticked and type it in, leave an email address. That email goes to our help desk as a ticket so we can reply about that feedback, and it is used for nothing else: it is not given to the venue, not added to any venue's list and never used for marketing. Venues see only how many players rated a night well, never the comments or who left them.

What a player's phone sends us without being asked. So that one phone is counted once, and so that a game cannot be flooded or a join forged, we keep a random identifier that the game stores in the player's own browser, and a scrambled (hashed) form of the phone's internet address rather than the address itself. Neither is used for advertising and neither is given to the venue. Both are removed from the venue's player list with the rest of its details; the random identifier also stays on any bingo ticket or bingo claim from that phone, as part of the record of that game. The scrambled profile key described above is kept with the profile until the profile is deleted.

Members lists. A club running a members draw can upload its own members list: a member number and a name for each member. Those people may never use venueplay themselves. The list belongs to the club, is held for that club alone, and is never used for marketing by us or shared with anyone else. It is used to run and record that club's draw. A member who wants to come off it should ask their club, which can remove them on the spot, or contact us at the address in section 14.

If a club wishes to, it can also upload each member's email, mobile, postcode and whether they want offers from the club. We hold these for the club, as part of its members list.

When a player at that club gives a mobile that matches a member on its list, we note for that club that the player is a member.

We keep a venue's copy of its players' details, and its members list, for as long as the venue holds an active venueplay account. When a venue's account has been closed for 90 days, the names, email addresses, mobile numbers, postcodes and offers choices on that venue's player list and members list are deleted, along with the identifiers described above and the names of members draw winners. Member numbers are kept. Purchase raffle entries and purchase raffle winner names are not part of this automatic deletion: we keep them for the venue's records and delete them when asked. A player's own venueplay player profile is not affected; the venue's player list keeps a private link to it, which goes when the profile is deleted.

Technical information

Like most websites, we automatically collect basic technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, and pages visited. We use Google Analytics for this. We also run the Meta Pixel on our public pages, which tells Meta that a browser visited us so we can show venueplay ads to venues who have looked at the site. It is not on the host console, the venue screens or the player pages. The Meta Pixel never receives a venue’s player list, or anyone’s name or email address.

Google Analytics also uses Google’s advertising features. These estimate an age range and a gender for visitors, and recognise the same person across their phone and computer, using the profile Google already holds for people signed in to a Google account. We only ever see this as totals for the whole site, never as an individual, and we never match it to a venue, a host or a player. You can turn it off for yourself at Google’s opt-out page or in your Google ad settings.

Our website also counts the steps of each visit, with the state or country, the kind of device and the site you came from, but never your name or internet address. Apart from a visit from one of our email links to venues (see "When we contact a venue about venueplay"), these counts do not identify you. If your browser sends Do Not Track, they are not recorded.

3. How we use your information

  • To set up and provide the venueplay service to your venue
  • To process your subscription and payments through Stripe
  • To provide support and respond to your enquiries
  • To send you service messages about your account, billing and the platform
  • To send you marketing about venueplay where you have consented, which you can opt out of at any time
  • To keep a player's venueplay player profile, so they are not asked to type the same details again
  • To improve and secure the platform
  • To meet our legal, tax and record-keeping obligations

4. Marketing and your consent

We only send marketing communications where you have agreed to receive them, and every marketing message includes a way to unsubscribe. We handle electronic marketing in line with the Spam Act 2003 (Cth). Opting out of marketing does not stop essential service messages about your account or billing.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers we use to run venueplay, and only so they can perform their function for us:

  • Stripe: payment processing and subscription billing
  • Supabase: secure database hosting for your account data
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, delivery and security
  • Resend: sending transactional and (where consented) email
  • Mobile Message (and ClickSend as a backup): sending sign-in codes by SMS. The codes that confirm a player's new mobile number are sent by Mobile Message only
  • Analytics providers such as Google and Meta, where enabled

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect our rights, safety or property.

6. Overseas disclosure

Some of the providers above store or process data on servers outside Australia (for example in the United States or the European Union). Where this happens we take reasonable steps to ensure your information is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

7. How we protect your information

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, including using reputable providers, encrypted connections and PCI-compliant payment handling through Stripe. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your personal information while you are a customer and for as long afterwards as we need it for legitimate business and legal reasons, including tax and financial records, which Australian law generally requires us to keep for at least five years. A player's venueplay player profile is kept until the player asks us to delete it (section 2).

9. Accessing, correcting and deleting your information

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it if it is wrong or out of date, or ask us to delete your venueplay player profile. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to make the site work and, where enabled, to measure how it is used. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as well without them.

11. Children

venueplay is a business tool intended for venue operators. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first using the details below and we will work to resolve it. If you are not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.
