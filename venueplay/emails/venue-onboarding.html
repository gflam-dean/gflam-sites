Two steps to your first night

1 Set up the screen.

Google TV or Android TV: get the venueplay TV app from Google Play, open it and type the six-character screen code from your host console. That is it, it remembers your venue.

Any other TV, a Fire Stick or a laptop on HDMI: open venueplay.com.au/tv in the TV's web browser, type the screen code, then go full screen with nothing covering the window. A page hidden behind another window slows down and shows RECONNECTING.
2 Sign in and start a game. On your phone, go to venueplay.com.au/app and we text you a code. Then start a game from the host console. The players' join code shows on the host console, and on the TV once a game is open.
