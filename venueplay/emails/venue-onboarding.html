The venue is set up and ready to run bingo, trivia, musical bingo, raffles and members draws. Billing is handled on the head office account, so there is nothing to pay or sign up for here. Everything below is the setup.

How you sign in

With your mobile number. No password to remember. Enter your mobile at venueplay.com.au/app and we text you a code. Head office adds each host's mobile from their account, so if the code does not come, ask them to add you.

Three steps to your first night

1 Set up the screen. Any TV works, a Fire Stick, a smart TV, or an HDMI cable from a laptop. Open the TV link below on the venue screen once and a join code appears for players.
2 Sign in on your phone or the bar laptop. That is the host console: you start the game, call it and see who is winning from there.
3 Run a test game. Play a quick round yourself, phone in hand, so it all feels familiar before a room full of people is watching.
