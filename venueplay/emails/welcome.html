A note on bingo in {{nobingo_places}}

In {{nobingo_places}}, only a not-for-profit organisation may run bingo, so number bingo is switched off for pubs and hotels there and you will not see it in your console. That is the state's rule, not a fault, and we would rather tell you now than have you go looking for it.

If a club or charity runs the bingo at your venue, just reply to this email and tell us who runs it. Once we have seen their licence or approval, we will switch it on for you.

Three steps to your first night

1 Sign in on your phone. Go to venueplay.com.au/app and enter your mobile. We text you a code, and you're in. Your host console shows your six-character screen code.
2 Add your hosts. In Account > People, add each host's name and mobile. We text them where to sign in, and they sign in with their own mobile.
3 Put venueplay on your TV. Go to venueplay.com.au/tv on your TV's screen and enter your six-character screen code. Keep it full screen with nothing covering it, or the TV can fall behind.

Your plan, in plain terms

You pay {{player_rate}} a month for each player in your plan. Your player limit is {{player_count}}, so that's {{payment_total}}. You can change it any time. Prices include GST.

Busy night? If more people turn up than your limit covers, each extra player that night is $2, and your host OKs it on screen first. If that happens three nights in a row, we move your limit up to fit the crowd, at your normal per-player rate.

To change your player limit, go to Account > Billing.

On an annual plan, added players are pro rata to your renewal. If you drop your maximum part way through the year, the lower maximum starts that day, and the value of the players you release for the rest of the year is held as a credit that comes off your next renewal on its own. There is nothing to claim. Your billing page shows what you are holding.

No lock-in. Cancel any time before your first payment and you are never charged.
