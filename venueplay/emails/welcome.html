Your plan, in plain terms

You pay per player, on the maximum players per session you set. Prices include GST.

If a night runs over your maximum, each extra player that night is $2. Your host sees what it costs and has to tap OK on the screen before the game starts, so nothing is ever charged without someone at your venue agreeing to it.

If you run over three times in a row, that is your crowd rather than a big night. The third one is $1 per extra player and we lift your maximum to the smallest of those three crowds, from your next invoice. It costs less than paying per head every week.

Changing your maximum is self-service in your account. Adding applies straight away and lands on your next bill. Dropping takes effect at your next renewal, so you keep what you have paid for. On annual, added players are pro rata to your renewal, and anything you drop is held as a credit rather than lost.

No lock-in. Cancel any time before your first payment and you are never charged.
