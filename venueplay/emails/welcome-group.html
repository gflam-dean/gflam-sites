Your plan, in plain terms

You pay per player, on the maximum players per session you set. Prices include GST.

If a night runs over your maximum, each extra player that night is $2. Your host sees what it costs and has to tap OK on the screen before the game starts, so nothing is ever charged without someone at your venue agreeing to it.

If you run over three times in a row, that is your crowd rather than a big night. The third one is $1 per extra player and we lift your maximum to the smallest of those three crowds. On monthly that starts from your next invoice; on annual the extra players are charged pro rata to your renewal, since your year is already paid for. It costs less than paying per head every week.

Changing your maximum is self-service in your account. Adding applies straight away and lands on your next bill. On monthly, dropping takes effect at your next renewal, so you keep what you have paid for. On annual, added players are pro rata to your renewal, and players you drop are released straight away with the rest of the year held as a credit rather than lost.

On an annual plan, if you drop your maximum part way through the year the lower maximum starts that day and we do not refund it, but the value of the players you release for the rest of the year is held as a credit and comes off your next renewal on its own. There is nothing to claim and nothing to spend. Worth knowing: the credit is the part of the year you have not used, so the sooner you drop players you are not getting, the more of it there is. Your billing page shows what you are holding.

No lock-in. Cancel any time before your first payment and you are never charged.

Three steps at each venue

1 Set up each screen. Any TV works, a Fire Stick, a smart TV, or an HDMI cable. Open that venue's TV link and its join code appears.
2 Add each host. Whoever runs the night at each venue gets their own login.
3 Run a test game. Play a quick round at each venue so it all feels familiar before the first real night.

Your screens

On each TV: open that venue's screen link, shown on its card above, once. Each one is venueplay.com.au/tv?venue=your-venue, and the screen remembers that venue after that, so next time the link is just venueplay.com.au/tv.

To sign in: you, your managers and your hosts all sign in at venueplay.com.au/app with a text code to your mobile, no app to install. One login switches between all {{venue_count}} venues.
