Good to know

Questions

When can I start?
Right away. Sign up before 30 September to lock in your New South Wales founding rate, get your free first month, and we'll help you get your first night running.
What does "founding venue" actually mean?
New South Wales venues that join before 30 September 2026 lock in $2.50 per player a month instead of $3, for life. It stays locked while you are subscribed. If you cancel, the founding rate ends for good, and rejoining later is at the standard rate.
Do players need to download an app?
No. They scan a code and play straight from their phone's browser. No app, no login, no fuss, which means more of the room actually joins in.
What kind of venues is it for?
Pubs, clubs, bowls clubs and RSLs: anyone running a regular entertainment night, or wanting to start one. If you've got a screen and a crowd, you're set.
How does billing work?
A simple subscription priced per player, per month, based on your venue's max players per session. Pay monthly at $2.50 a player, or yearly for $2.30 a player, and your founding pricing is locked for life either way. New venues get a free first month, then billing recurs at your founding rate. Cancel any time before your first payment and you are never charged.
What if more players turn up than my plan?
No surprises, and nothing happens without your host. Set your max to your usual room. The moment a night runs over it the host sees exactly what it costs and has to tap OK before the game starts, so you are never billed for a big night nobody agreed to. Each player above your max is $2 for that night, and you know that number from day one. Go over three weeks in a row and that is your crowd, not a big night: the third one is half price at $1 a head and we lift your max to the number you hit all three times. On monthly that starts from your next invoice. On annual, where the year is already paid for, the extra players are charged pro rata to your renewal, and if you later drop back down the unused value is held as a credit for the following year. It works out cheaper than paying per head every week. You can drop back to your average any time from your account, and we always email before your bill ever changes.
Can I change my max players?
Any time, from your account. Adding players applies straight away so you are covered that night, and the extra is on your next bill. Dropping down takes effect at your next renewal, so you keep what you have paid for until then. On an annual plan, added players are charged pro rata to your renewal date, and if you drop your max the unused value is not lost: it is held as a credit against your next renewal.
What do I need to run it?
A screen, an internet connection, and a way to get the display onto that screen: a smart TV with a browser, a streaming stick, or an HDMI cable from a laptop or tablet all work. Players bring their own phones. For a rock-solid night we'd point you toward a stick or a cable over wireless casting, since that doesn't lean on venue wifi.
Is there a lock-in contract?
No lock-in. It's a rolling subscription you can cancel anytime. Your founding price stays locked for as long as you're with us, and if you cancel before your first payment, you're never charged.
What support do I get?
Founding venues get hands-on onboarding to get your first night running, plus ongoing support if anything comes up. The whole thing is built so your bar staff can run it without a tech in the room.
Can players win prizes?
Yep, you set the prizes, exactly like you do now. Bar tabs, meat trays, members jackpots, vouchers, whatever suits your venue. VenuePlay runs the game and calls the winners; the prizes are yours to decide.
Is players' data safe?
Players join with just a name, no account, no login, nothing they have to hand over. Anything captured is opt-in and handled under Australian privacy rules.
If I run trivia at different venues, do I keep the players' details?
No, and that's by design. A venue's player data belongs to that venue. If you run games at a venue you don't own, VenuePlay only ever collects a player's first and last name for the game itself: it can't switch on email, mobile, postcode or marketing opt-in, so there's no marketing list to take. Nothing is held on the side or handed over later. Each venue's opt-in players stay with that venue.
Who's behind it?
VenuePlay is built by Gflam Touring, who run bingo, live music and entertainment across Australia. It's the same show experience, built into a platform any venue can run.
