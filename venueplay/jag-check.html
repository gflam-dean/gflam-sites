Check the Joker

For Jag the Joker jackpots run on VenuePlay.

How the proof works. When a jackpot starts, our server picks the Joker's card at random and locks it away with a long secret key that nobody at the venue can see. At the same moment it publishes a fingerprint made from three things: the game, the card and the key. That fingerprint is on the TV from the first week.

When the Joker is found, the card and the key are revealed. This page puts them back through the same recipe in your own browser. If the Joker had moved, even by one card, the fingerprint would come out completely different.
