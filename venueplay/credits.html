Credits and licences

Last updated: 20 August 2026

VenuePlay is built on our own software, but parts of it stand on work other people shared freely. This page credits them and states the licences that apply, as those licences require.

Trivia questions

Part of the VenuePlay trivia bank comes from two open question sets:

  • Open Trivia Database (opentdb.com)
  • OpenTriviaQA (github.com/uberspot/OpenTriviaQA)

Both are used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International Licence. We have changed the material: questions have been fact-checked and corrected, re-categorised, given difficulty ratings, and in places reworded for an Australian room. The compiled question collection is shared under the same licence.

The rest of the bank is written by us for Australian venues, and questions you write for your own venue stay yours. Both are covered by the intellectual property section of our Terms of Service.

Music

VenuePlay does not supply or license music. Where you run musical bingo, you are responsible for holding the appropriate music licences for your venue, for example APRA AMCOS and OneMusic Australia. Song details shown on screen are metadata only.

Questions about any of this

If you believe something here is credited incorrectly, or should be credited and is not, email hello@venueplay.com.au and we will fix it.
