## 1. Stripe: does an annual venue's overage ever get invoiced?  (money, unverified)

**The worry.** When a venue goes over its player count we write a Stripe
`invoiceitem`. A pending invoice item sits on the customer until something
finalises an invoice. On a monthly plan the next cycle sweeps it up. On an
**annual** plan the next cycle is up to twelve months away, and if the venue
cancels before then the item is discarded and we are never paid for it.

I cannot check this: it needs your Stripe dashboard.

**What to look at.** Stripe → Customers → filter to a venue on an annual plan
that has gone over. On the customer page, look for **"Pending invoice items"**.

- If pending items are listed with no upcoming invoice date inside the next
  month, the money is at risk and we should invoice overage immediately at the
  point of charge instead of parking an item.
- If they are attached to an upcoming invoice with a near date, this is fine and
  I will close it off.

Tell me which of the two you see and I will do the rest.

## 2. Broadcast signing - NOT YOURS. Leave it off.

Recorded here so it is not raised again as an open item.

**What it is.** A game runs over a channel named after the join code, and that
code is on the TV in giant letters. Anyone who knows it can connect to the same
channel and shout at it: a fake winner, fake balls, or push the screen to the
ads. Signing means the host console signs every message and the screens ignore
anything unsigned.

**You never create a key.** The console mints one in the browser the first time
a host opens it. "9 of 19 venues have a key" only means nine venues have had a
host sign in since the feature shipped. There is no action here and never was.

**Nothing sensitive is on those channels.** Checked 5 Sep: no email, no phone,
no opt-in data on any broadcast. Game events and players' first names. So this
is a griefing risk, not a data breach, and not a Privacy Act question.

**Why it stays off.** Enforcing means a venue's screens drop unsigned messages.
If a console has not minted a key, or a host is on a stale page, the TV drops
EVERYTHING and the room stares at a black screen. That is both likelier and
worse than what it prevents, which needs a punter willing to open a browser
console, work out the channel name and craft a valid message mid-game.

It needs a quiet venue and someone free to watch a whole game with enforce still
off. There is no such evening before launch. The SQL that does it sits ready at
`venueplay-backend/supabase/venueplay-62-broadcast-enforce-turn-on.sql` for
whenever there is.

## 3. Touring: the Save button is dead, and publishing a show is still a push

Two separate things here, so they are separated.

### 3a. Publishing a show page - you do NOT need to touch GitHub any more

`manage.html` generates `<key>.html` and pops an alert telling you to upload it
to the touring folder on GitHub. That instruction is out of date for how we work
now. `touring/` lives in the same repo as everything else and deploys from
`main`, and I push to `main` directly.

So: generate the page, then **send me the file, or just tell me the show
details**, and I will commit it to `touring/` and push. It is live a couple of
minutes later at `www.gflamtouring.com.au/<key>`. Nothing for you to do in
GitHub at all.

(I checked the live site against the repo today: `manage.html` on
www.gflamtouring.com.au is byte-for-byte the copy in this repo, so nothing has
drifted and there is no backlog of unpushed touring work.)

### 3b. Saving a show is broken, and has been silently

The public tables were locked at some point. I probed all four today with the
public key:

| table | read | insert | update | delete |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shows | works | refused 42501 | refused 42501 | refused 42501 |
| experience | works | refused 42501 | refused 42501 | refused 42501 |
| ticket_milestones | works | refused 42501 | refused 42501 | refused 42501 |
| tour_categories | works | refused 42501 | refused 42501 | refused 42501 |

Locking them was right. `manage.html` is the casualty: it wrote straight to those
tables with the public key, so **Save now fails** on every show, milestone and
experience edit. Reading still works, which is why the public tour pages are fine
and this went unnoticed.

It should not go back the way it was. The "PIN gate" is a four digit code whose
SHA-256 sits in the page source - ten thousand guesses - and it was cosmetic
regardless, because the writes went out under the public key with or without it.

**Your call:**

- **(a) A small touring Worker of its own.** Recommended. You create one Worker
  in Cloudflare, paste one file, set one password as a secret. manage.html then
  calls it instead of the database. I deliberately do NOT want to bolt this onto
  `venueplay-api`: that Worker bills a live venue, and re-pasting it for a
  touring feature risks the thing we can least afford to break. About an hour.
- **(b) Leave it read-only** and edit shows in the Supabase table editor. Free,
  but it becomes a manual job every time.

Say (a) or (b) and I will get on with it.
