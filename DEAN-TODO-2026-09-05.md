## 1. Stripe: does an annual venue's overage ever get invoiced?  (money, unverified)

**The worry.** When a venue goes over its player count we write a Stripe
`invoiceitem`. A pending invoice item sits on the customer until something
finalises an invoice. On a monthly plan the next cycle sweeps it up. On an
**annual** plan the next cycle is up to twelve months away, and if the venue
cancels before then the item is discarded and we are never paid for it.

I cannot check this: it needs your Stripe dashboard.

**What to look at.** Stripe → Customers → filter to a venue on an annual plan
that has gone over. On the customer page, look for **"Pending invoice items"**.

- If pending items are listed with no upcoming invoice date inside the next
  month, the money is at risk and we should invoice overage immediately at the
  point of charge instead of parking an item.
- If they are attached to an upcoming invoice with a near date, this is fine and
  I will close it off.

Tell me which of the two you see and I will do the rest.

### 3b. Saving a show is broken, and has been silently

The public tables were locked at some point. I probed all four today with the
public key:

| table | read | insert | update | delete |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shows | works | refused 42501 | refused 42501 | refused 42501 |
| experience | works | refused 42501 | refused 42501 | refused 42501 |
| ticket_milestones | works | refused 42501 | refused 42501 | refused 42501 |
| tour_categories | works | refused 42501 | refused 42501 | refused 42501 |

Locking them was right. `manage.html` is the casualty: it wrote straight to those
tables with the public key, so **Save now fails** on every show, milestone and
experience edit. Reading still works, which is why the public tour pages are fine
and this went unnoticed.

It should not go back the way it was. The "PIN gate" is a four digit code whose
SHA-256 sits in the page source - ten thousand guesses - and it was cosmetic
regardless, because the writes went out under the public key with or without it.

**Your call:**

- **(a) A small touring Worker of its own.** Recommended. You create one Worker
  in Cloudflare, paste one file, set one password as a secret. manage.html then
  calls it instead of the database. I deliberately do NOT want to bolt this onto
  `venueplay-api`: that Worker bills a live venue, and re-pasting it for a
  touring feature risks the thing we can least afford to break. About an hour.
- **(b) Leave it read-only** and edit shows in the Supabase table editor. Free,
  but it becomes a manual job every time.

Say (a) or (b) and I will get on with it.
