## Changed (copy only, still NOT RUN through the gate)

**1. The front page told search engines you have nine games.**

```
<meta name="description" content="Bingo, trivia and seven more, ...">
```

Bingo plus trivia plus seven is nine. The page itself lists ten, numbered 01 to 10, and
the heading says "Ten of them". That description is what appears under the link in every
search result and every time somebody pastes it into a chat. Now reads "eight more".

**2. The booking page had no description at all.**

`/start` is the page every ad and every cold email points at, and it had no
`description` and no Open Graph tags. A blank snippet in search, and a blank card when
the link is shared. Added, matching the front page's wording.

## Findings, for you to decide

**3. The Worker supports two games the site never mentions.**

`FORMATS` in the Worker holds twelve:

```
bingo90, trivia, musical, draw, howwell, headstails,
whohere, photos, truths, playlist, charades, guesswho
```

Ten of those map exactly to the ten on the front page. Two do not:

* `musical` (musical bingo)
* `playlist`

So either they are built and you are selling ten while shipping twelve, or they are
half-built and a host who reaches them finds something unfinished. Both matter, in
opposite directions. I could not tell which without running the host console, and I was
not going to guess on a live product overnight.

**If they work, they are free marketing.** Musical bingo is the game VenuePlay venues
like most, and "ten games" becoming "twelve" costs nothing to say.

**4. PartyPlay's emails carry no ABN. VenuePlay's do.**

Every PartyPlay email goes through one shell, and its footer is:

> PartyPlay is made by Gflam Group Pty Ltd on the Gold Coast. partyplay.com.au

VenuePlay's footer is:

> venueplay.com.au · Gflam Group, ABN 35 679 383 049

Your own standard is that the ABN belongs in every email footer, and these are
commercial emails to consumers: a purchase receipt, a licence, an album link. This is
one line in `emailShell` and it fixes every PartyPlay email at once.

**I have not made that change**, because of the open question in
`MORNING-2026-09-12.md`: you said that number is VenuePlay's ABN. I am not putting an
ABN on a consumer receipt until you tell me which one is right. Give me the number and
this is a one line change covering both products.

## What I did NOT look at, and would want to

The money and delivery path, properly: checkout, the Stripe webhook, and
`sendLicenceEmail`. PartyPlay is delivered by **one email**, and the gate already carries
a check called "Nobody paid for a party and got nothing" precisely because a failed send
leaves a buyer with a charge and no party.

That is the exact shape of the fault we spent last night on in VenuePlay: an email that
threw, a catch that swallowed it, and nothing written down, so the only witness was the
customer. I would look there first, and I would look for the same three things:

1. does the send record what happened, or only that it was attempted
2. is there any path where the money is taken and the catch hides the failure
3. does anything read a Stripe field that the 2025 API no longer sends

`check-stripe-fields.py` already covers point three for PartyPlay's Worker: it is in the
sweep and it came back clean last night.
