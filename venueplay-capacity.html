The measured ceiling is 155 calls a second. At 500 venues you would be running at 91% of it with no headroom, which is why the room server belongs there rather than later. At 1,000 the joins alone are 200 a second, so the join has to come down from 12 calls to about 3.

5,000 venues at 50 seats and $3 a player is about $750,000 a month of revenue. Infrastructure is around 0.1%.

By then the join has to be down to about 2 calls. That is the one real piece of work between here and there.

What to do, in order

  1. Supabase Sydney to Pro, about $25 USD a month. Mostly for the backups: Free has none at all, and seventeen paying venues sit on a database with no restore point. It also lifts 10 venues to 29 and stops the project pausing itself after a quiet week.
  2. Cache the 8 second lobby poll. Biggest single win, 29 venues to about 550, and cheap. That endpoint hands every phone on the same code the same four fields, so caching it at Cloudflare for three seconds removes roughly 99% of the dominant load.
  3. Turn the room server on. Already built, already tested, took 500 phones in one room. Gets you to about 775 and moves live game traffic off Supabase entirely.
  4. Make the join cheaper. 12 calls down to 3 is what takes you past 3,000 venues. This is the real engineering job, and nobody has started it.

The waiting phone is the dominant load and the cheapest thing to fix. Ninety phones standing in lobbies is the entire project's budget, before anybody has answered a single question.

One busy room

Fine to about 80 phones. Visibly slowing at 90. Falls over at 100. Because the ceiling is shared across the whole project, a hundred phones in one pub is felt by all seventeen venues at once.

The speed win from the Sydney move is real, and it only exists while the system is quiet. Under a crowd Sydney is slower than Singapore was, because it runs out of capacity sooner.

Database size is a non-issue

35 MB of the 500 MB free ceiling, and 14 MB of that is the 37,678 question trivia bank, which barely grows. You would not see 500 MB for years.
