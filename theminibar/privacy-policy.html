Privacy Policy.

Last updated: April 2026

The Mini Bar and the Gflam Group of Companies are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information.

We are bound by the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Information we collect

Contact information - name, phone number, email address and postcode when you sign up, make an enquiry or purchase tickets.

Payment information - held securely by our payment processors. We do not store card details.

Usage information - browser type, IP address and pages visited to help us improve our service.

Communications - any messages or enquiries you send us.

How we use your information

We use your information to provide our services, respond to enquiries, send updates you have opted into, and improve our websites and products.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties.

Marketing communications

If you sign up to our mailing list, we may send you news, offers and updates relevant to The Mini Bar. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.

Cookies

Our websites use cookies to improve your experience. See our Cookies Policy for details. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access. Our data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption.

Access and correction

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and to request corrections. To do so, contact us at hello@gflam.com.au.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@gflam.com.au or write to Gflam Group of Companies, Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia.
