Monthly Payout Run

Runs on the 1st of the month for the PREVIOUS month. Per property, rolled up per chain: total sales, the property's own commission %, and the commission The Mini Bar pays that venue. Simulated trading is shown so the run is complete, and any live kiosk sales for the month are folded in.

Compliance & Audit Log

Every age check, OTP verification, cap check, refusal, payment, vend and restock, timestamped and append-only. This is the property's responsible-service due-diligence record and doubles as evidence for OLGR and hotel partners. Refusals are highlighted in red.

Global Policy Defaults

The fleet-wide responsible-service defaults. A property can tighten these on its own record (Fleet page). Changing a default changes how every machine verifies age and caps alcohol unless a property overrides it. A change is picked up by the kiosk on its next load.

Handle with care. The buffer age forces a photo-ID check when a face estimate lands under it. The daily cap is the maximum alcoholic drinks per verified mobile per day. The stay window is how long a mobile stays verified before a fresh code is needed. Every change here is written to the audit log as due-diligence evidence.
