The Machine

Not your average
vending machine.

The Mini Bar is a smart, touchscreen vending machine purpose-built for hotels and hospitality venues. With built-in age verification, it sells alcohol, soft drinks, snacks and essentials — 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

One machine per floor. No stockroom. No staff time. No theft. No wastage. Just a beautifully stocked, always-available retail experience your guests will love — and you collect the commission.
