# Gflam Brand Voice Guide

## Universal rules (every brand)

- Plain Australian English. Warm, direct, zero corporate filler.
- **No em dashes.** Commas, colons, or restructure.
- Short sentences. One idea per line in social posts.
- Dates/times unambiguous: "Fri 24 Oct, 7pm". Venue name always in full first mention.
- Every post has ONE call to action (buy tickets / book now / sign up / message us).
- Never invent facts: prices, dates, venues, artist names come from Dean, Supabase, or the brand skill. If unknown, leave a clearly marked [PLACEHOLDER] and raise a question via the ops flow.
- Accessibility: emoji as garnish not structure; no all-caps sentences except single-word hype ("SOLD OUT").
- Any claim about availability ("selling fast", "last tickets") must be true — check with Dean if unsure.

## Per-brand voice

### Drag Bingo at the Bowlo
Cheeky, camp, high-energy, inclusive. Puns welcome. Queens named with love (Vollie Lavont, Mandy, ShuShu). Audience: locals, hens groups, birthday tables, bowls club regulars. Content style that converts (proven by Meta results): performer-led creative for cold audiences, event-detail carousels for warm retargeting. Ticket link is Moshtix. Always include venue, date, time, price. Meals/kitchen notices matter to this crowd. Reusable video scripts: modular body + venue-specific tags, dates as overlays.

### Gflam Touring
Professional, capable, national-scale. Speaks to venues, councils, promoters, corporates. Proof-led: numbers, logistics, safety, delivery. No hype words ("world-class") without evidence. B2B tone, LinkedIn-appropriate.

### Gflam Vending
Practical B2B. Speaks to hotel/venue managers: revenue per square metre, zero staff overhead, service reliability. Short, ROI-first.

### The Mini Bar
Its own palette AND its own tone: premium, playful, event-luxe. Cocktails and occasions. (Golden Gaytime and Iced Vovo cocktails are staples.)

### Gflam Party Hire
Family-friendly, fun, local (Oxenford/Gold Coast). Speaks to parents, schools, community groups. Clear pricing and delivery zones. Sugar-and-smiles energy without being childish.

### VenuePlay
Venue-facing SaaS. Confident, simple, benefit-led: "Run a packed Tuesday. Every Tuesday." Never "drag" in VenuePlay copy. Pricing always per player. See the `venueplay` skill for the hard copy rules before writing anything.

### Who Gives A Cluck (WGAC)
Hen rescue charity (Upper Main Arm, NSW). Warm, community-hearted, gratitude-forward. Volunteer recognition, adoption drives, Cluck n Learn events. Never salesy; donations/volunteering asks are gentle and specific.

## Formats

**Facebook event post**: hook line → 2–3 detail lines (what/where/when/price) → social proof or fun line → CTA + link. 60–120 words.
**Instagram caption**: hook → short body → CTA → 3–8 relevant hashtags (local + niche, no hashtag walls).
**EDM**: subject <45 chars, preview text set, one hero message, one CTA button, unsubscribe always present (Spam Act).
**Ad copy (Meta)**: primary text ≤125 chars before the fold; headline ≤40 chars; one benefit, one CTA. Pair with the winning creative pattern for that brand.

## Output rules for agents

Marketing content is NEVER posted directly. Every piece goes to the ops approval queue (see `ops-team` skill) with: brand, format, target platform, suggested publish date, and the final ready-to-paste text. Dean approves in the HQ dashboard.
